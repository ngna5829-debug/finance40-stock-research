When we criticise human institutions as they are, and at-

that, whatever the strain, they remain unaltered, we are im-

certain limit.        We have not been supposing that the currency
systems which we have been considering were capable of re-
maining in all circumstances unaltered.                 We have recognised
that at a time of crisis the law may be broken or even amended.
We have seen the statutory limitation of the Bank of Eng-

banks of issue charging a premium on gold when an excessive
export is threatened. We have shown that the greatest
normal strain to which the currency system of a country can
be exposed is that which arises from the occurrence of a crisis

    How does it come about that Government finance tres-

Government only raises money by taxation, by loans which

and services so diverted it has to compete in the market just
as if it were a large private firm. The Government is a trader,
selling law, order, and the organisation of society to its
customers, the taxpayers.
