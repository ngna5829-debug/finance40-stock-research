This  one  case  in  point  tells  the  whole  tale,  and  it  is

other  instances.  If  legitimate  trading  business,  in

man  to  earn  a  living  at  it,  cannot  be  carried  on  without
adequate  capital,  how  is  it  possible  that  pure  specu-

and  only  then  in  some  cases  under  circumstances  to
