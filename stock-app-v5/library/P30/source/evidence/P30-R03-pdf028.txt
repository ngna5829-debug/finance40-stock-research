As  every  rule  has  its  exception,  so  in  speculation
are  there  a  few  professional  experts  who  succeed  at
it  as  a  business.  What  is  contained  in  these  pages  is
not  for  the  expert,  who  is  well  able  to  take  care  of
himself,  but  for  the  ordinary  haphazard  operator.
The  professional  speculator,  who  has  the  right  sort  of
head,  sufficient  capital,  patience,  perseverance,  cool-
ness, and  a  business-like  aptitude  for  laying  down  the
elaborate  machinery  that  is  necessary  for  mercantile
