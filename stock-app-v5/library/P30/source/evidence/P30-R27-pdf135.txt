The  "  turn  "  comes  in  the  second  rank  of  obstacles
which  stand  between  the  speculator  and  the  goal,  or
profit,  which  it  is  his  aim  to  reach,  and  is  the  most
formidable  of  the  fixed  and,  it  may  be  said,  inevitable,
elements  arrayed  against  him  at  the  start.  When  a
speculator  enters  the  markets,  therefore,  he  has  to  do
his  share  of  keeping  both  the  broker  and  the  jobber,
and  that  not  only  when  he  commences  his  operations,
but  also  when  he  finishes.  There  is  the  "turn"  to  be
paid  on  going  in,  and  also  on  coming  out.  The  same
