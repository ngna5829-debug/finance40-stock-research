discrimination  is  necessary.  The  different  classes  of
investors  must  be  passed  through  the  speculator's
machine  like  the  threshed  corn,  and  when  the  husks
and  dust  have  been  winnowed  from  the  solid  grain,  he
proceeds  to  classify  them,  and,  as  far  as  possible,  learn
their  taste  in  the  matter  of  investments.
